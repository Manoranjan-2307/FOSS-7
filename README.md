## 📜 Thirukkural 619
> **தெய்வத்தான் ஆகா தெனினும் முயற்சிதன்** <br>
> **மெய்வருத்தக் கூலி தரும்**

### 💡 Explanation
Even if fate or circumstances completely block your path to success, the physical effort and hard work you put in will never go to waste. Your determination acts as its own reward, bringing you strength, growth, and unexpected gains for the strain your body endured.

---

## 🎨 Our Frontend Idea
We are building a highly interactive, cinematic **"scroll-telling"** experience tailored for 5th graders. 

* **The Journey:** As the user scrolls down the page, each of the 7 words of the Kural is revealed one by one alongside fun emojis and modern, easy-to-understand hints.
* **The Finale:** The experience culminates in an epic **"Reverse-Snap"** animated finale, where all the scattered words magnetically assemble together on the screen. 
* **The Goal:** This visually impactful approach ensures the core message—that *effort beats luck*—leaves a lasting and memorable impression on young minds.
